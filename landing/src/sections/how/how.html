From capture to countermeasure

Every number below comes from one real VoIP capture run through the live dashboard.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Upload a packet capture from your VPN gateway. IKE handshakes are read in the clear to audit the crypto the two ends agreed on. ESP traffic is only measured: how big each packet is, and when it arrived.

  2. 2

    Classify

    Sizes and timing feed a calibrated random-forest model. On this capture it names the traffic as VoIP with 86.2% confidence, and SHAP values show which features gave it away: the packet count first, then the gaps between packets.

  3. 3

    Countermeasure

    The simulator pads every packet to full size and scores the flow again. Sizes stop leaking; timing doesn't. The shadow keeps its rhythm, so confidence only falls to 68.0%, at a 435.5% bandwidth cost. The report says so plainly.
